Model, privacy & limitations

What runs on your device

This is a browser adaptation of HumanFallDetection by Mohammad Taufeeque and Samad Koita, based on commit 0f9fa006. Its original trained two-layer, 48-unit LSTM weights are retained. Its five pose/motion features and fall-warning filter are ported to JavaScript.

The original Python OpenPifPaf pose estimator is replaced with Google MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Lite for browser compatibility. Pose confidence, bounding boxes, tracking, camera frame rates, and missing-body handling differ. Therefore this adaptation does not inherit any published accuracy result. It has not been validated for real-world safety monitoring.

What the overlays mean

Green means a body is tracked or a posture is inferred, not that the person is safe. Standing and Sitting are experimental geometry labels, not trained class names. Gray means warming up or insufficient pose information. Amber means evaluating movement or accumulating down-posture evidence. Red means a possible fall or sustained-down warning. Tracking IDs are temporary, not personal identities. Hidden limbs are not reconstructed.

The model has seven internal activity classes, with class zero treated as fall by the upstream application. Raw model outputs are not calibrated probabilities. The browser reports each tracked person separately, unlike the original first-person-only display logic. Brief missing observations may preserve tracking/posture evidence without adding evidence time; longer gaps reset it. Hidden head landmarks pause the LSTM while independently valid torso/leg checks continue.

Automatic movement checks and optional room tuning

Separate experimental rules run without room marking. A stable standing/sitting baseline followed by rapid hip drop, reduced vertical body span, torso rotation and 2 seconds of observed down posture can trigger Possible fall. Eight seconds of sustained down evidence can trigger Person may be down, even without seeing a transition. Brief unclear frames pause evidence instead of immediately erasing it. These timings and thresholds are not validated safety guarantees.

A horizontal torso alone is not enough: visible leg geometry and body proportions also matter. This is a 2D posture estimate, not impact or floor-contact detection. Intentional lying on the floor, bed or sofa may trigger a warning. Optional furniture exclusions can suppress warnings when the torso overlaps the region; optional floor regions limit posture checks. Regions clear when the session ends and must be redrawn after camera movement. Camera movement without an aspect-ratio change is not automatically detected. Wrong regions can suppress a real event. Diagnostics expose evidence and blocking reasons, not proof of safety.

Camera & privacy

Camera access begins only after Enable camera and your browser’s permission prompt. No microphone access is requested. Images and model predictions are processed in browser memory, not uploaded, recorded, or stored. Model/runtime assets are served from this site; the hosting provider receives normal page/asset requests and may retain access logs, but this app sends no camera imagery or predictions.

Stop camera, leaving the page, or switching tabs releases the camera and ends detection. Your operating system may also suspend processing when the phone is locked or the browser is backgrounded. No background notifications or contact escalation are provided. Local alert sound starts enabled, can be turned off, and depends on browser audio permission and device volume. Test that it is audible.

Use safely

Mount the camera still, disable automatic framing/zoom, and keep the full body and floor visible. Lighting, blankets, furniture, slow collapses, partial bodies, multiple people, movement toward the camera and camera movement can cause missed detections or false alarms. Good placement does not eliminate these limitations. A handheld phone aimed at someone is not a stable safety-monitoring setup. Never rely on this prototype instead of normal care or emergency systems. Safely staged tests should involve capable, consenting adults only.
